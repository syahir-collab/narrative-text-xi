01 · Foundation

What Is Narrative Text?

English

Narrative text is a type of text that tells a story or a sequence of events. It often aims to entertain readers, engage their imagination, and may communicate a moral lesson or cultural value.

Narratives commonly include characters, a setting, events, a problem or conflict, and an ending. Traditional narratives can reflect the beliefs, customs, and values of a community.

Bahasa Indonesia

Narrative text adalah jenis teks yang menceritakan sebuah cerita atau rangkaian peristiwa. Teks ini sering bertujuan menghibur pembaca, membangun imajinasi, dan dapat menyampaikan pesan moral atau nilai budaya.

Narrative biasanya memiliki tokoh, latar, rangkaian peristiwa, masalah atau konflik, dan akhir cerita. Narrative tradisional dapat mencerminkan kepercayaan, kebiasaan, dan nilai suatu masyarakat.

🎯

Purpose

To entertain, engage imagination, and sometimes communicate lessons or cultural values.

Menghibur, membangun imajinasi, dan terkadang menyampaikan pelajaran atau nilai budaya.

🧩

Characteristics

Characters, setting, events, conflict, resolution, and storytelling language.

Tokoh, latar, peristiwa, konflik, penyelesaian, dan bahasa penceritaan.

🌍

Types

Legends, folktales, fairy tales, myths, fables, and other traditional or imaginative stories.

Legenda, cerita rakyat, dongeng, mitos, fabel, dan cerita tradisional/imajinatif lainnya.

02 · Traditional Stories

Legends

English

What is a legend?

A legend is a traditional story associated with a particular person, place, or event. Legends may contain extraordinary or supernatural elements and can have cultural or historical associations. A legend should not automatically be treated as a factual historical record.

Bahasa Indonesia

Apa itu legenda?

Legenda adalah cerita tradisional yang berkaitan dengan orang, tempat, atau peristiwa tertentu. Legenda dapat mengandung unsur luar biasa atau supernatural serta memiliki kaitan budaya atau sejarah. Legenda tidak otomatis dapat dianggap sebagai catatan sejarah faktual.

Characteristics / Karakteristik

🏞️ Place-based

Often connected to a particular place.

👤 Person/Event

May focus on a notable person or event.

✨ Extraordinary

May include supernatural or unusual elements.

🧬 Tradition

Passed down across generations.

🎭 Culture

Can reflect cultural beliefs and values.

💡 Lesson

May communicate a moral or cultural message.

Examples of Indonesian Legends

English

Malin Kundang: A traditional story from West Sumatra about a son who becomes wealthy and rejects his mother. The story is commonly associated with the importance of respecting parents.

The Legend of Lake Toba: A traditional Indonesian story associated with the origin of Lake Toba and Samosir Island. It includes a promise, a broken taboo, and a dramatic transformation of the landscape.

Bahasa Indonesia

Malin Kundang: Cerita tradisional dari Sumatra Barat tentang seorang anak yang menjadi kaya lalu menolak ibunya. Cerita ini sering dikaitkan dengan pentingnya menghormati orang tua.

Legenda Danau Toba: Cerita tradisional Indonesia yang berkaitan dengan asal-usul Danau Toba dan Pulau Samosir. Cerita ini memuat janji, pelanggaran pantangan, dan perubahan alam yang dramatis.

03 · Traditional Stories

Folktales

English

What is a folktale?

A folktale is a traditional story shared within a community or culture. Folktales were often passed down orally before being written down. They may entertain audiences while preserving cultural ideas, social values, beliefs, or practical wisdom.

Bahasa Indonesia

Apa itu folktale?

Folktale atau cerita rakyat adalah cerita tradisional yang hidup dalam suatu komunitas atau budaya. Cerita rakyat sering diwariskan secara lisan sebelum dituliskan. Cerita tersebut dapat menghibur sekaligus mempertahankan gagasan budaya, nilai sosial, kepercayaan, atau kebijaksanaan praktis.

Let's Explore a Legend

The Golden Lake

Orientation: Long ago, in a quiet valley surrounded by green hills, a young farmer named Arga lived with his mother. They were poor, but Arga was known for his kindness and hard work.

One morning, while Arga was working beside a river, he found a small golden stone. An old traveler appeared and told him that the stone was a gift, but he must never use it to harm another person.

Complication: Arga took the stone home. His neighbor became jealous and tried to steal it. In anger, Arga forgot the traveler's warning and chased the neighbor into the hills. The stone fell into a spring.

Suddenly, the spring grew wider and deeper. Water covered the valley, while the hills remained above the water. Arga realized that his anger had caused the disaster.

Resolution: Arga apologized to the people who had been affected and spent many years helping them rebuild their homes. The community eventually called the place Golden Lake because the water sometimes reflected the golden light of sunrise.

Cultural / Moral Value: A gift should be used responsibly, and anger can lead to harmful decisions.

Danau Emas

Orientasi: Dahulu kala, di sebuah lembah tenang yang dikelilingi perbukitan hijau, seorang petani muda bernama Arga tinggal bersama ibunya. Mereka miskin, tetapi Arga dikenal baik hati dan rajin.

Suatu pagi, ketika bekerja di dekat sungai, Arga menemukan sebuah batu emas kecil. Seorang pengembara tua muncul dan mengatakan bahwa batu itu adalah hadiah, tetapi Arga tidak boleh menggunakannya untuk menyakiti orang lain.

Komplikasi: Arga membawa batu itu pulang. Tetangganya iri dan mencoba mencurinya. Karena marah, Arga melupakan peringatan pengembara dan mengejar tetangganya ke perbukitan. Batu itu jatuh ke sebuah mata air.

Tiba-tiba, mata air menjadi semakin lebar dan dalam. Air menutupi lembah, sementara perbukitan tetap berada di atas permukaan air. Arga menyadari bahwa kemarahannya telah menyebabkan bencana.

Resolusi: Arga meminta maaf kepada orang-orang yang terdampak dan menghabiskan bertahun-tahun membantu mereka membangun kembali rumah. Masyarakat kemudian menyebut tempat itu Danau Emas karena airnya kadang memantulkan cahaya keemasan matahari terbit.

Nilai Moral/Budaya: Sebuah pemberian harus digunakan secara bertanggung jawab, dan kemarahan dapat menyebabkan keputusan yang merugikan.

Characters

Arga, his mother, a traveler, and a neighbor.

Setting

A valley, river, hills, and a spring.

Conflict

Jealousy and anger lead to a harmful decision.
